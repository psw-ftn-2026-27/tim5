### Hero candidates considered on 11 November 2026

All three original pages explicitly link the free Unsplash License. The comparison
sheet is kept locally in `.workspace/research/home-hero-candidates.jpg`, in this order:

1. [Samuel Arkwright — Golden Hour in the Dolomites](https://unsplash.com/photos/jagged-mountain-peaks-illuminated-by-golden-sunrise-light-WCWDT67Hr2s/): detailed peaks but a brighter, flatter sky and less depth behind the text.
2. [Marek Piwnicki — Breathtaking Dolomites](https://unsplash.com/photos/jagged-mountain-peaks-bathed-in-warm-sunset-light-TB1IaqcYNyM): strong warm light and wide composition, but a tighter view of the mountain faces.
3. **Daniel Mirlea — selected**: layered ridges, mist, golden light and prominent peaks to the right leave room for the heading on the left.

The two alternatives are not shipped as application assets. The previous Mario
von Rotz Hero photograph has been replaced. This selection was made from the
photographers' original source pages, without reference to other student projects;
exclusive use across those projects cannot be verified.
